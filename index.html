Curious first, designer second

I'm a UX researcher, UI designer and web developer from East Java. I like projects where I can follow an idea from the first interview to the shipped screen: asking the right questions, shaping the experience around the answers, and building it so it works the way it was designed.

UX research

UX design

User flows, information architecture and prototypes that turn research findings into clear journeys.

UI design

Visual systems and polished interfaces, presented in decks and design files a team can build from.

Web engineering
