Approach

From question
to shipped screen

Design decisions are only as good as the understanding behind them. My process moves from research to experience design, to interface and finally to code, and I document each step so the thinking is as visible as the result.

UX
research

Interviews, surveys and usability tests to learn what people actually need, before anything is drawn.

UX
design

User flows, information architecture and prototypes that turn research findings into clear journeys.

UI
design

Visual systems and polished interfaces, presented in decks and design files a team can build from.

Web
engineering
